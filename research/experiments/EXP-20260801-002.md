## 3. Research question

### Hypothesis

A paper-only coordinator can bind each fresh exact-pair quote to an already frozen non-odds candidate, recompute the existing robust value decision, and append an idempotent shadow event before the internal deadline without changing the candidate or enabling any order path.

### Null hypothesis

The coordinator cannot reliably preserve the frozen candidate identity, quote freshness, decision deadline, and idempotency contracts while producing timely paper decisions.

### Racing mechanism

Later races require their own near-post market observation because available prices change during the day. The horse combination remains determined by the pre-market racing model; only the separately governed value observation is refreshed for the exact frozen ticket.
