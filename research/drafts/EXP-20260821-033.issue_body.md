## Hypothesis and minimal variant

Remove `race_winner_prior_strength`, `race_top3_prior_strength_mean`, every
contaminated fitted-output descendant, draw/horse-number inputs, market/result inputs,
and all features without strict predraw as-of proof. Rebuild exactly one fixed
`SimpleRaceRanker` variant with a strict chronological outer OOS. Lower apparent
performance than the old leaky model is acceptable; leakage, schema mismatch,
as-of failure, or incomplete 70-runner inference is not.

No model family, hyperparameter, feature subset, threshold, seed, or postdraw search
is permitted.
