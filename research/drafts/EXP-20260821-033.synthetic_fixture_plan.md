# EXP-20260821-033 synthetic fixture plan

Status: proposal draft only. No fixture implementation or execution is authorized.

Variant: `leakfree_predraw_baseline_v0` only.

After a GitHub-backed `APPROVED_TO_PREPARE <proposal_scope_digest>` is verified,
preparation may implement synthetic unit fixtures for the following contracts.
No fixture may read the real race history, target card, model artifact, odds, result,
or outer-OOS rows.

## Pre-registered cases

1. Current-result mutation: changing `確定着順`, `target_score`, `target_win`, and
   `target_top3` for the prediction race leaves all 88 features and scores unchanged.
2. Future-row invariance: appending later-dated rows leaves every earlier feature and
   score unchanged.
3. Race-boundary shift: two races on the same date cannot leak through row-order
   shifting; source event time must be strictly earlier.
4. Draw isolation: adding or mutating `枠番`, `馬番`, `前走馬番`, and draw descendants
   leaves schema and scores unchanged.
5. Market isolation: adding or mutating odds, popularity, market, payout, ROI, BUY,
   value, and stake columns leaves schema and scores unchanged.
6. Default-deny: one unknown generated feature causes hard failure.
7. Missing required current-card field: hard failure rather than silent imputation.
8. Debut horse: no-prior history fields follow the frozen zero semantics.
9. Missing previous fourth corner: neutral `0.5` is used and counted in QA.
10. Early-move fallback: exact order is `1C -> 2C -> 4C`.
11. Category parity: train-frozen levels and `__MISSING__` reproduce identically in
    inference; unseen levels follow one fixed rejection/mapping policy.
12. Schema parity: names, order, dtypes, fill policy, and category dictionary match
    byte-for-byte between synthetic train and inference matrices.
13. Non-finite rejection: NaN after finalization and positive/negative infinity fail.
14. Chronology: any race overlap or non-monotone `train < validation < calibration < outer`
    boundary fails.
15. Outer seal: the outer loader cannot be called before model/config/calibrator hashes
    are frozen.
16. Target-universe contract: a synthetic five-race/70-runner manifest joined by
    `race_id` and `horse_id` yields no duplicates or missing runners; empty draw fields
    are valid.
17. Ranking contract: each race has finite scores and exact ranks `1..n`, with ties
    resolved by `(score desc, horse_id asc)` while `horse_id` is not a feature.
18. Safety isolation: importing production, Champion, BUY, candidate, notification,
    order, or stake modules fails the fixture.
19. Safety flags: `formal_buy=false`, `send_order=false`, and `stake=0` are immutable.
20. Single-variant contract: any second variant, feature subset, threshold, seed, model
    family, or hyperparameter candidate is rejected.

## Prepare-stage stop

Preparation stops after implementation plus synthetic unit-test evidence. Real-data
feature building, fitting, validation, calibration, outer OOS, target inference, and
old-model comparison require a separately frozen canonical run scope and a distinct
GitHub-backed `APPROVED_TO_RUN <run_scope_digest>` comment.
